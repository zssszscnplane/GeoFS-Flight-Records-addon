# GeoFS-Flight-Records-addon
Flight Records addon for GeoFS . By exporting flight records , you can download the flights you finished . By importing flight records , you can see all of the flights you have finished . Come and have a try ! 

# GeoFS 航班记录插件
GeoFS的飞行记录插件 。 通过导出航线记录，您可以下载您完成的飞行航线。通过导入航线记录，您可以看到您已经完成的所有飞行航线。快来试试吧！
